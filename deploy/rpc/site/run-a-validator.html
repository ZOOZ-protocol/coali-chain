    7. 




Good to know
Bon à savoir


    
  
  • If your node produces no block for about 12 minutes, it is set aside automatically so it cannot slow the network. Once it runs again, come back with proofOfTrust › goOnline() in Developer › Extrinsics, signed by your validator account.
    • 
  
  • To leave: proofOfTrust › withdraw().
    • 
  
  • Updates: docker pull / reload the new image when announced, then docker restart coali. Your keys stay in the coali-data volume — never delete it.
    • 
  
  • Never share your account recovery phrase. The Coali team will never ask for it.
    •
